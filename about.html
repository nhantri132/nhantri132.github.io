About

I’m a gameplay designer with almost a decade of design experience across a wide range of titles. I want to make games that are viscerally entertaining and emotionally meaningful.

My strengths are organizing and communicating large reference libraries for design teams, and combining that research with my design experience to solve problems.

How I work

  1. Reference

    I’ve played over 1,300 games and keep a library of combat references. When a problem comes up, I already know what’s been tried and what’s worth borrowing.

  2. Translate

    I boil that research down for animators, engineers, producers and artists, in terms each of them can use, so the whole team works from the same picture.

  3. Build

    I implement and iterate on it myself, owning the enemy systems end to end and building them from the ground up, from prototype to ship.
